Research Interests

I work on both theoretical and computational problems in stellar astrophysics, with particular emphasis on oscillations and fluid dynamics. My current interests include:

  • Asteroseismology and stellar oscillations: p modes, g modes, rapidly rotating stars, mode identification, and structural inference.
  • Tides and binary stars: tidally excited oscillations, heartbeat binaries, nonlinear mode coupling, wave breaking, and mass-transfer systems.
  • Astrophysical fluid dynamics: gravity and inertial waves, wave–mean-flow interaction, rotating fluids, magnetohydrodynamics, and magneto-Coriolis waves.
  • Multidimensional stellar oscillations: finite-element calculations in centrifugally distorted stars, full self-gravity, differential rotation, magnetic fields, and tidal forcing.
  • Inverse problems and statistical inference: asteroseismic kernels, adjoint sensitivities, Bayesian inference, time-series analysis, and spectral reconstruction.

Numerical tools used in my research include MESA, GYRE, FEniCSx, Nek5000, and Dedalus.
